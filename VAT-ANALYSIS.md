## PART 1 — HER GOLD, SŪTRA BY SŪTRA

### Sūtra 3 — The Uncreated Book (ubhayapaṭṭoddhaṭṭana → mahāśūnya)
**The teaching:** Entrance into the great void *by bursting open the two boards* — the space
BETWEEN dualities is one's own nature. The manuscript's boards matter, not its content.
**Her gold:**
- The Chumma Saṅketa Prakāśa story: Niṣkriyānanda Nātha falls into trance at the glance of
  siddha Gaṇḍanādana; the uncreated book (akṛta puṣṭaka) is shown; the two boards = the two
  breaths + immanent and transcendent goddess; "he must hear this asunder and behold the
  ultimate void that lies between them **as his own nature**" → sudden enlightenment.
- Lakshman Joo's ladder of the two boards: subject/object → breaths (exhale/inhale, anava) →
  knowledge/action (śākta) → **prakāśa / vimarśa** (divine, Śiva-Śakti).
- "The word *uncreated* is important — not made by human agency."
- Only the central prāṇa is called *madhya* → practice of the center.
**Cites:** Chumma Saṅketa Prakāśa (Krama oral), Anantaśaktipāda vṛtti, Lakshman Joo,
Vāmadeva's handwritten note.
**Our passages:**
- vṛtti verbatim: `vatulanathasutra.txt` (p.4–5: prāṇa/āpāna upper/lower board exegesis).
- Śiva Sūtra 1.5 *udyamo bhairavaḥ* + spanda passages (sivsv_1.5) — the "bursting" as utthāna.
- Krama corpus: `kriyakramadyotika/`, `pingalamata/`, `pratisThalaksanasara/`.

### Sūtra 4 — Devouring the pairs (yugmagrāsa → niravakāśa)
**The teaching:** Swallow the pairs → establishment in pure consciousness "where there is no
occasion for anything else." Not opposites — *complementarities*, worked out through the
tattva scheme (25 common / 36 Śāḍviśa).
**Her gold:**
- "The object of experience is ever-present in the experiencing subject, and the subject
  ever-present in the object." (her thesis sentence)
- Even the vikalpa/nirvikalpa pair must be swallowed — "it is still a duality" (her
  surprise-emphasis; the standard spiritual distinction gets cancelled).
- Swallowing = neither destruction nor exclusion nor subordination, "but integrating the two
  in a **coincidence of opposites which is the very dynamic nature of consciousness**."
- Kula/kaula swallowed into unity; NOT intellectual — "a few who receive the vision shown by
  an excellent guru."
**Cites:** Anantaśaktipāda, Lakshman Joo's manuscript translation (niravakāśa), tattva scheme.
**Our passages:**
- vṛtti: `vatulanathasutra.txt` p.5–6 (grāhya/grāhaka per element, bhogye'pi bhoktā sadaiva
  tiṣṭhati — "in the object the experiencer always stands").
- Tattva system: `tattvaprakasa_bhojadeva/`, `siddhantasara/`, `tattvasamgraha_sadyojyotis/`.
- Subject-object co-presence → our recognition essays (gochema `FORMALIZATION-OF-RECOGNITION.md`).

### Sūtra 7 — Four forms of speech (vāk chatuṣṭaya → svara)
**The teaching:** In the arising/subsiding of the four speeches, the original sound of
consciousness (svara) is revealed. Language = graded descent from the absolute.
**Her gold:**
- Three similes of unity-in-multiplicity: **peacock's egg** (all colors in one), **banyan
  seed** (whole tree in a seed), **fruit containing many seeds** — every full name contains
  all four levels.
- Three levels: **svara** (vibration of consciousness) → **mahānāda** (mystical sound) →
  **āhata/anāhata** (struck — all music and speech).
- Practice: "awareness of the beginning and end of any utterance, any thought" — enlightened
  speech "has a gravity and depth derived from universal I-consciousness."
- The musician's message: every sound can carry to its transcendent source.
**Cites:** Anantaśaktipāda's "wonderful concise summary," Śaiva śāstras generally,
Lakshman Joo's svara translation.
**Our passages:**
- Śiva Sūtra 2.7 (verified): *akārādivisargānta svaraṣoḍaśagarbhiṇī* — the 16 svaras in
  Anuttara; *ato 'kārahakārābhyām aham ity apṛthaktayā* — "aha" without separation.
- Śiva Sūtra 1.6:4–6: *yayaiva svātmacidbhittau… parapramātṛviśrāntiparyantaspandarūpayā*.
- `spandapradipika/` (parā/parāpara refs), `nadakarika/` (Nāda Kārikā!), `matrkabhedatantra/`.

### Sūtra 8 — Three essences (rasa tritaya → spontaneous Brahman)
**The teaching:** By relishing the three essences the unbound Brahman *emerges spontaneously*.
Three supports (mūlādhāra, payodhāra, ādhāra) = creation / maintenance / reabsorption —
explicitly **NOT chakras** ("Muladhara doesn't have here the meaning it has in Hatha Yoga").
**Her gold:**
- "Paiyotara" (support of milk) — "I'm not finding any text of tantra or yoga, it's quite
  unique used here" — consciousness nourishes like a mother the child.
- pīṭha = "a plain wall on which the image of the whole universe is projected."
- The process "is not to be done by will, but happens spontaneously by an inner inspirational
  impulse" (pratipha ullasa — Trika's signature).
- Kha-mudrā = khecarī = "Abhinavagupta: the highest of all mudras."
- The realized one "is not only enlightened for himself, but who can enlighten the whole world."
- Lakshman Joo's three rasas = will / knowledge / activity, in three moments of tasting:
  admit the universe in own nature → expand in delight → grasp intensely non-separation.
**Cites:** Anantaśaktipāda (stress: oral transmission, "probably you cannot understand it
without direct transmission from the guru"), Abhinavagupta, Lakshman Joo.
**Our passages:**
- Mudrā: `kriyakramadyotika/`, `svacchasvayambhuva`/`suksmasvayambhuva/`,
  `isanasivagurudevapaddhati/` (khecarī verified in file list).
- Spontaneity → Tantrāloka corpus + our `pushing-tantraloka/` notes.
- Projection-wall → pratyabhijñā recognition texts (Utpaladeva `utpaladeva-power-of-action`).

### Sūtra 10 — Twelve currents (senses transfigured)
**The teaching:** Mystical experience is NOT cut off from sense experience — the twelve
currents (5+mind, 5+intellect) rise and the senses are *transmuted into divine energies*
(sun and rays).
**Her gold:**
- Three stages: outward flow (natural) → **internalization** (Katha Upaniṣad *parāñci khāni…*
  avṛta-cakṣu, sage turned eyes inward) → **rising/transformation** (u daya) — the third stage
  compressed in this sūtra's brevity.
- "It is not only the yogi who attains perfection, but his **divinized senses attain the state
  of pure consciousness which radiates throughout the universe**."
- Called **Mahāvākya**, like the Upaniṣads' fundamental sentences.
- Lakshman Joo variant reading (Vadasya Vaha…), svātantryā śakti gloss.
**Cites:** Katha Upaniṣad, Anantaśaktipāda, Lakshman Joo, Kashmir edition variants.
**Our passages:**
- Upaniṣads in corpus: `atharvasiraupanisad/`, `kaivalyaupanisad/`, `siraupanisad/`,
  `sivaupanisad/`, `tattvaprakasa_bhojadeva/` (matched avṛta refs).
- Śiva Sūtra 3.6 pratyāhāra sequence (verified): *pratyāhāra iti prokto bhavapāśanikṛntanaḥ*.

### Sūtra 11 — Five modes of activity (caryā pañcaka)
**The teaching:** Read from the Siddha/jīvanmukta's perspective: five ecstatic modes of the
divinized senses, absorbing their objects into consciousness. Waveless absorption.
**Her gold:**
- The five: anāśrita (supportless) / avadhūta (thrown off restrictions) / unmata (mad) /
  savabhakṣa (all-consuming) / mahāvyāpaka (omnipervasive) — mapped to ear, eye, smell, taste,
  touch. "Behavior of a mindless lunatic who does not discriminate acceptable/unacceptable."
- **Even śāmbhava upāya is excluded** — "any upāya has a movement, even śāmbhava" (her
  surprise-notes: the state is beyond all technique, even the highest).
- Lakshman Joo's five acts as stages of ascent: silence → agitative movement → agitated state
  → confirmation → supreme silence; and "agitation is not kṣoba — it is **spanda**. Consciousness
  in this tradition is dynamic, not totally static."
**Cites:** Anantaśaktipāda, Lakshman Joo (chāryā vs karma distinction: "when we enter through
all the senses in God consciousness, then that act is called caryā").
**Our passages:**
- Śiva Sūtra 1.5:3 *udyamo 'ntaḥparispandaḥ pūrṇāhambhāvanātmakaḥ* (inner spanda full of
  aham) — the exact Spanda point she makes.
- `mrgendratantra/`, `svacchandodyota/`, `pancarthabhasya_kaundinya/` (caryā exegesis).

### Sūtra 12 — Beyond good and evil (mahābodha → mahāmukti) ⭐ ETHICS
**The teaching:** The famous Tantra-claim, **with its condition**: the yogi must have evolved
consciousness *after* the whole prior practice — the sūtra sits deliberately at position 12,
after all duality-overcoming, right before the peak.
**Her gold:**
- **"The excellent hero who while living experiences this freedom… will not be reborn."**
  (jīvanmukta + rebirth line — our rebirth-study hook.)
- **Mahāmukti** = "transcends both bondage and liberation" — has NO opposite; "clear like an
  amalaka fruit in one's hand."
- Lakshman Joo's double: under ignorance we also feel "nothing is right and nothing is wrong"
  — "but this type of feeling is bound to lead us to degradation." Same appearance, opposite
  direction. The condition: "we have to realize the eternal existence of God Consciousness."
- kṛtaka-anupāva = "not made, not artificial, without instruments" → she translates
  **spontaneous**.
**Cites:** Anantaśaktipāda, Lakshman Joo.
**Our passages:**
- `janmamaranavicāra/` (Vāmadeva, KSTS 19 — literally birth-and-death inquiry; rebirth tie-in).
- `moksakarika/`, `ratnatrayapariksa_*`, `pancarthabhasya_kaundinya/` (karma doctrine),
  `sivadrsti/`.
- Pairs with our rebirth shelf: Beth Upton notes + Analayo + Dyczkowski Karma-and-Rebirth (mp3
  on disk).

### Sūtra 13 — Silence that speaks (akathanā → mahāviśmaya mudrā) ⭐ RECOGNITION
**The teaching:** Silence is not empty — it "contains and transcends language, is the
culmination of all language." Two rival readings of the final sūtra.
**Her gold:**
- Anantaśaktipāda: negative **A** + letter-A theory — four sounds: struck (āhata) → unstruck
  (anāhata) → struck-from-unstruck → beyond both (akathanā, unspeakable).
- Lakshman Joo: speechless speech (parā) + **Mahāviśmaya mudrā** — THE moment:
  "What for I was working? What for I was doing practice of yoga? What for I had discarded all
  the activities of the world? What for I had gone to the feet of my master? **All what I feel
  now, I was already feeling that.**"
- Khaśvara / khasvaram = "luminous resonance of the void," = harmonious unity (sāmarasya) of
  revelation; "resonance of the void" (her translation choice).
- The colophon: the 13 sūtras "contain the essence of the six darśanas, the four tantric
  traditions, and all the unions of Siddha and Yoginī" — the text's own claim to be a digest.
- Her own close: "one becomes equally speechless, and lands in the great silence."
**Cites:** Anantaśaktipāda colophon + verse, Śvetāśvatara *viśmaya* (she connects to "highest
states of yoga are full of wonder"), Lakshman Joo.
**Our passages:**
- Śiva Sūtra 1.6:17 *turyaṃ nāma paraṃ dhāma tadābhogaś camatkriyā* (camatkāra = wonder).
- `sivasutra/` vismaya/ camatkāra refs (verified in file).
- **This is recognition**: astonishment at the already-given → gochema
  `recognition/ESSAY-RECOGNITION-FELT-RE-COGNITION.md`, `DAIMON-CONTACT-AS-RECOGNITION.md`,
  Utpaladeva galley PDF.

## PART 2 — THE ESSAY SKELETON (her sequence IS the argument)

Her eight lectures, in her order, already form a complete arc. Title candidates:
**"The Space Between the Boards"** — a Vātūlanātha essay in Bäumer's architecture.

1. **Enter the between** (S3) — the void is not behind experience but *between* every pair;
   the uncreated book = what no one made (thesis).
2. **Swallow the pairs** (S4) — subject and object co-inhabit; even thought/thoughtlessness
   must go; integration not annihilation.
3. **Let speech descend** (S7) — four voices from one silence; unity-in-multiplicity similes;
   every word is a fall and a return.
4. **Taste the world** (S8) — rasa, nourishment, the wall-screen of consciousness; the mudrā
   of the sky; spontaneity over will.
5. **Transfigure the senses** (S10) — not withdrawal but transformation; three stages
   (out-turned → in-turned → risen); the divinized senses radiate.
6. **Act beyond method** (S11) — five ecstatic modes; even the highest technique dissolves;
   spanda, not stasis.
7. **The condition on the beyond** (S12) — beyond good and evil is earned and conditioned;
   mahāmukti has no opposite; the ignorance/gnosis twin (ethics pivot + rebirth line).
8. **Silence speaks** (S13) — "I was already feeling that" — the recognition punchline;
   resonance of the void.

**Cross-text counterweights (our corpus, one anchor each):**
1–2: vṛtti p.4–6 (verbatim) + recognition formalization essay.
3: Śiva Sūtra 2.7 mātṛkā-svara sequence + nadakarika.
4: tattvaprakāsa / siddhantasara tattva lists + Utpaladeva.
5: Katha Upaniṣad quote + Śiva Sūtra 3.6 pratyāhāra.
6: Śiva Sūtra 1.5 spanda + mṛgendra/svacchanda caryā.
7: janmamaranavicāra + karma triad (Dyczkowski Karma-and-Rebirth mp3, Analayo, Beth).
8: Śiva Sūtra 1.6 camatkāra + our recognition essays.

**Assembly recipe:** quote her English (transcripts, ear-checked against audio) as the
connective voice → drop Sanskrit vṛtti lines under each section as the primary-source spine →
one cross-text passage per section from our corpora → close on her last sentence
("one becomes equally speechless, and lands in the great silence"). Nothing is lost: each
sūtra also has her own "in the next session" seams we can quote or cut.
