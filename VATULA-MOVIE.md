## The thesis (v2)

The text's own claim: realisation is **sudden, uncaused, without method**
(svātantrya — sūtra 8/10/11). So the film does not *depict* sudden realisation —
**it stages it, repeatedly, on the viewer.** The form enacts the doctrine: the
structure carries what the words refuse, and what the words refuse is exactly
*the jump*. 0/A/B/C remains the vessel (question → gold → depth → texture);
the engine inside it manufactures **attention-opening events** — the
"what the hell is going on?" gap in which realisation has somewhere to land.

## The core loop (every engineered moment)

```
POINTER (question / oddity)  →  GAP (held silence or unsourced sound)
                              →  LANDING (the clip line arrives and fills the gap)
```

The viewer's attention snaps open at the GAP; the teaching sentence lands into
open attention = the sudden-realisation opportunity. THREE-CLOCKS law still
holds: the landing usually arrives a beat *after* curiosity peaks — or
(deliberately) a beat *before* the voice says it, so the image opens the
question the words then name.

## The jolt vocabulary (unsourced sounds = the user's animal noise)

Sounds with **no visible source** — they come from "outside the film":

- **forest/animals** (the text's world: sacred grove): langur alarm shriek ·
  crow cut-off mid-call · deer · owl at the wrong hour · cicada wall suddenly
  stopping · jackal at night
- **near-human**: a distant laugh · a chant fragment cut mid-word · one struck
  bell from another room · a breath in the left ear channel
- **grammar-violations** (break *our own* rules — the sharpest class):
  the wood tick where *no* page/section turn happens · the bowls struck with
  **no bell on screen** · a single 1/12s black flash mid-scene · the same shot
  played twice (déjà-vu) · a page **un-turning** (reversed)

**Anti–jump-scare law (this is contemplative, not horror):** curiosity, never
fear. Never a volume spike (ceiling = bell family +3 dB, room-mixed so it feels
like it came through the window). Every jolt must be **answerable within 30 s**
by a real clip line (the teaching catches the confusion) — an unanswerable
jolt is a wound, an answerable one is a kōan.

## Jolt budget by layer (0/A/B/C — the restraint is the design)

| layer | role | events | rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — questions (spine)** | opener-heads | 1 per question head (≤5) | pattern: question → GAP+unsourced sound → first A-clip answers |
| **A — gold** | **maximum opportunity** | ~6–8 across the cut | never two within 90 s (refractory); pointer-gap precedes each gold arrival |
| **B — depth** | few, huge | 2–3 only, at *collapse* beats (many facts → one image) | B's suddenness = complexity snapping, not sound |
| **C — texture** | bath | **exactly one** — the last, before the seal | restraint inversion: as the walk thickens, the jolts vanish |
| cross-cutting | grammar-violations | ≤4 whole film | own-rule breaks only; never during chant (chant rule) |

Counts + refractory distance are **mechanical** — `p0_check`-style: budget
violations = FLAG. Flow passes add the qualitative question: *"did any sound
make you curious rather than startled?"*

## Pointers (the other half of the engine)

- **Deictics** ("this", "here", "now") in questions and bridges must **resolve**
  inside the film (shiva's deictic gate, same law) — a pointer that binds =
  attention turns on itself; a dangling one = frustration.
- **Pointer-gap-landing triads** are authored into the timeline like everything
  else: `kind: jolt` / `gap_extra` blocks with the anchor named in the comment —
  the sheet stays the single source of truth; QA counts budgets from it.
- **The marker pulse stays regular** — the whole point of surprise is violation
  of the steady 13-beat grid: constant rhythm + rare irregularity = maximum
  grab (psychophysics, and it's also just music).
