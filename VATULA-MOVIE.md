## Logline

Thirteen oral teachings of a text that says its own insight **cannot be taught
in mere words** — taught anyway, by two voices. What the words refuse, the
*structure* carries: the cut, the pause, the pulse. The film is the transmission
happening in the place language admits it fails.

## What the structure hands us (the obvious things)

1. **Sūtra 3 is the book.** "Entry into the great voidness by **bursting open
   the two boards** of the puṣṭaka" — the manuscript lives *between two colored
   boards*. The custom film's central image = boards opening; the standard's
   book-film = the closed state. **The two works rhyme: one reads it closed,
   the other bursts it open.** (We already own the etch diagram of the pustaka.)
2. **The two voices ARE "the pair"** (sūtra 4, *devouring the pair*): Bäumer
   and Hareesh alternating is our pair dissolving into one sense — the
   alternation itself becomes visible mechanism, revealed once (analayo's
   ballerina-winding: show the two-track structure in a single shot, never explain).
3. **Word-edges** (sūtra 7, VĀK — awareness at the edge of the word): our edit
   grammar already lives there — every cut was silence-snapped to word ends.
   The film makes its own edits legible: **section cuts land on the wood mark,
   phrases end into air.**
4. **No-method as editorial doctrine** (svātantrya, sūtra 8/10/11): cuts arrive
   "without cause" — no dissolve where a cut serves; grace = the cut itself.
   Ready scenes: the rendered **shaktipata grace-spectrum pack (13 scenes)**.
5. **Silence-not-empty** (sūtra 13 + S5 fruit) **inverts analayo's Path 1**:
   their black = cessation; our black **hums** (the long bell = fullness).
   Coda-black with presence.
6. **Thirteen wood markers = the edit metronome.** Steal `THE-SOUND-MECHANISM`
   wholesale: all movement cuts land on the marker pulse — the sheet's timing
   *is* the film's heartbeat (signature timing made visible).
