## Design laws (from the owner)
1. **Structure emerges from the audio** — clustering → convergence → the spine
   falls out; never hand-picked first (question measurement precedent, §5 of notes).
   **The spine = the sections, and each section IS A QUESTION** (owner, corrected
   2026-10-25): master question first ("What is realisation?"), then the sub-
   questions the clusters ask. Section names are never slot-labels — always the
   question the units in it answer.
   **Standalone-clarity law (owner, 2026-10-25):** each question must make sense
   on its own — no insider coinages (rejected: "what is the between?"), no
   shorthand, no dependence on other section questions. A stranger hearing it
   cold must know exactly what is asked. **The spine = the most fundamental
   progression possible.** Locked set:
   1 What is realisation? · 2 What is the self? ·
   3 How does realisation happen? · 4 What does realisation do to experience? ·
   5 What is the fruit of realisation?
2. **Three essays, Russian dolls** — A ⊂ B ⊂ C (FORMULA, append-only), collated
   under the spine sections and ORDERED properly within each section.
3. **ESSAY 0 = THE SPINE ITSELF (owner re-correction, final):** the complete list
   of spine questions, **each with its compressed response** — the whole essay in
   miniature (5 gospel beats, question → interlinear → plain → her answer).
   The video plays ALL of Essay 0 at each layer head → the spine is heard 4×,
   each time followed by that layer's expansion. (The S1 micro-session built
   earlier = BEAT 1 of 5 — form is right, scope was wrong.)
   Beat shape: `[“What is realisation?”] → [pause] →
   [“Realisation is …”]` — one minimal, beautiful answer, ~10–25s, spoken by
   Bäumer (now) or Lakshmanjoo (when $0 audio lands). **It opens every version,
   every time — so it must be the best line we have.** (Analayo's analogue: seed →
   1 sentence (the motto) → 1 word ("stilling"). Ours is the motto-equivalent.)
   **Acceptance test (owner correction): the line must COMPLETE the sentence
   “Realisation is ___”.** A line about how attainment *arrives* (becoming vs
   getting) answers a different question — rejected for Essay 0 even if A-grade.

## BUILD ORDER LAW (owner, added 2026-10-25 — correction after B overrun)
**ESSAY 0 FIRST.** Build the SPINE in miniature — all five questions each with its
compressed answer (the gold dust) — before anything else; everything grows
OUTWARD from it: essay0(the 5-Q spine) → A (sections at speed, anchored on
the same answer) → B (A + depth that EXPANDS the essay0 answer) → C (A+B + texture).

overrun. B-length rule of thumb: B ≈ A × 1.6–2 (FORMULA: 20–40m); every added B
clip must pass the hold-up test: *does it deepen the essay0 answer?*

question→pause→plain→answer, 25–30s each) + `manifest.json`; lock =
`build/essay0_lock.json` (owner pick) — gates via verify.sh G12/--ship.
