# Vatulanatha sutra 9 + vrtti of Anantasaktipada (Muktabodha M00099, KSTS 1923)
# source: patalacheckpoints/data/corpus/sources/vatulanathasutra/vatulanathasutra.txt

p. 12) 	devīcatuṣṭayollāsena sadaiva 
svaviśrāntyavasthitiḥ || 9 ||

	devīcatuṣṭayaṃ kṣuttṛḍīrṣyāmananākhyam | tatra ca sarvagrāsanirata-
tvāt kṣudeva mahāsaṃhāraḥ | sarvaśoṣakatvāt tṛḍeva saṃhāraḥ | īrṣyā 
dvayaprathāpādikā grāhyagrāhakaparigrahagrathitā sthitirūpā | mananā ca 
saṃkalpavikalpollāsarūpā sṛṣṭiḥ | etadrūpasya devīcatuṣṭayasya ca 
'ullāsena' ghasmarasaṃvitpravāhapravṛttyā prathanena 'sadaiva' sarvakālaṃ 
pratyekaṃ cāturātmyenodyogābhāsacarvaṇālaṃgrāsavapuṣā svasvarūpā-
vasthitiḥ pañcamapadātiśāyinī niravakāśasaṃvinniṣṭhā sthitetyarthaḥ || 9 ||
	ityanena sūtreṇa devīcatuṣṭayakathākramaṃ prakāśya, idānīṃ 
dvādaśavāhacakrarahasyaṃ nirūpyate
