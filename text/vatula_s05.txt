# Vatulanatha sutra 5 + vrtti of Anantasaktipada (Muktabodha M00099, KSTS 1923)
# source: patalacheckpoints/data/corpus/sources/vatulanathasutra/vatulanathasutra.txt

siddhayoginīsaṃghaṭṭānmahāmelāpodayaḥ || 5 ||


	siddhāśca yoginyaśca tāḥ siddhayoginyaḥ viṣayakaraṇeśvarīrūpāḥ | 
tāsāṃ saṃghaṭṭaḥ saṃgamo grāhyagrāhakobhayasaṃśleṣaḥ 
parasparāgūraṇa-krameṇāliṅganam | tena āliṅganena sadaiva 
'mahāmelāpodayaḥ' mahāmelāpasyā-hantedantātmakadvayavigalanāt 
niruttaracidvyomni satataṃ mahāsāmarasyātmakasya sarvatra pratyakṣatayā 
udayaḥ samullāso bhavati ityarthaḥ | vedyavedakadvayāprathana-pravṛttyā 
paramādvayasamāveśaḥ sarvatrāvasthita ityuktaṃ bhavati || 5 ||

	ubhayavigalanena sadaiva mahāmelāpodayamuktvā, tadanu 
kañcukatrayollaṅ-ghanena niruttarapadaprāptiṃ kaṭākṣayanti
