# Vatulanatha sutra 12 + vrtti of Anantasaktipada (Muktabodha M00099, KSTS 1923)
# source: patalacheckpoints/data/corpus/sources/vatulanathasutra/vatulanathasutra.txt

mahābodhasamāveśātpuṇyapāpāsaṃbandhaḥ 
|| 12 ||

'mahābodhaḥ' ca jñātṛjñānajñeyavikalpasaṃkalpakāluṣyanirmukto 

p. 15)	niḥśamaśamāniketanirdhāmadhāmaprathātmakaḥ paratarajñānasva-
bhāvaḥ kramākramottīrṇatvāt mahāgurubhiḥ sākṣātkṛtaḥ | tasya 
'samāveśaḥ' akaraṇakrameṇa yathāsthitasaṃniveśena tyāgasvīkāraparihāra-
taḥ satatamucyutavṛttyā tadrūpeṇa sphuraṇaṃ | tasmāt 
'mahābodhasamāveśāt' puṇyapāpayoḥ śubhāśubhalakṣaṇakarmaṇordva-
yoḥ svaphaladvayavitaraṇaśīlayoḥ 'asaṃbandhaḥ' asaṃśleṣaḥ asaṃyogaśca 
anavarataṃ jīvata eva vīravarasya apaścimajanmanaḥ kasyacitsarvakālamakṛta-
kānubhavarasacarvaṇasaṃtṛptasya bhavabhūmāveva 
bandhamokṣobhayottīrṇamahāmuktiḥ karatalāmalakavat sthitetyarthaḥ || 12 ||

	svasvarūpaprāptipūrvakaṃ puṇyapāpatiraskāracarcākramamuktvā, 
idānīṃ svarasiddhamaunakathāmuddhāṭayanti
