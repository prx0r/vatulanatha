# GRACE — assembly document (v1, 2026-10-25)

**Concept:** *"Realisation is received."* A documentary-essay where **Bäumer's Vātūlanātha
series (~120 min) is the payload**, carried inside our own frame: our narration (cited
scholarship) + Tantrāloka ch. 13 + the already-rendered śaktipāta animation. She never
appears "as an upload"; the film is ours, the teaching is hers.

**Title options:** GRACE — What Is Grace? · Realisation Is Received · The Adept Only Opens

**Central thesis (ours, already written):** Grace is not a thing transferred (no saṁkrāma);
it is consciousness's own freedom (svātantrya) turning back inward — the adept only has to
*open*; everything else is done by grace. → `gochema/library/recognition/pushing-tantraloka/LOGICVID-session-grace-reversal.md`
